2. **s10 doesn't wire s05/s06/s08/s03/s04 into the LLM dispatch chain.** It only imports s02 (model client) and s07 (rollout). The full glue (gate → policy → sandbox → exec) is laid out in `docs/{zh,en}/s_full-integration.md` step 11 as a 100-LOC reader exercise. The architecture is right; the wiring is what's missing.
3. **s05 OnFailure** policy variant is defined as an enum value but its retry semantics aren't implemented. Documented as a gap.
4. **Context compaction** (codex-rs/core/src/compaction.rs) is not modelled. Listed as Appendix B exercise #2.
5. **Guardian nested approval** (codex-rs/core/src/guardian/) is not modelled. Listed as Appendix B exercise #3.
